MY VALUES

What matters to me.

Values are the principles I try to use when deciding what deserves my time, effort, attention, and trust.

WHAT I STAND FOR

The principles I want my life to reflect.

Independence matters to me. I want to be capable of making my own decisions, solving my own problems, and building a life that is not completely dependent on other people.

Knowledge matters because understanding gives me more choices. I would rather understand how something works than simply accept that it works.

Honesty matters even when the truth is uncomfortable. I do not want to build my identity around pretending to be something I am not.

Discipline matters because motivation is unreliable. There will always be days when I do not feel like doing something. I still want to be capable of doing it.

Competence matters to me. I want to become genuinely capable at the things I choose to pursue instead of being satisfied with simply appearing capable.

Privacy and personal freedom are also important to me. I believe people should have room to think, explore, make decisions, and control the direction of their own lives.

STILL LEARNING

Values are easier to claim than to live.

These are the principles I currently believe matter to me. Whether my actions consistently match them is another question. I am still learning how to turn beliefs into behavior.
