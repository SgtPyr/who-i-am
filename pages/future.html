THE FUTURE

Who I am becoming.

I have goals, plans, and things I want to build. I also know that the person I become will be shaped by what I learn and what I choose along the way.

WHERE I WANT TO GO

I want to build a life that belongs to me.

I want to become capable enough to create things instead of always depending on other people to create them for me.

Technology is one of the areas where I want to keep growing. I want to understand programming, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, automation, and the systems behind the tools I use.

I want to build software that is genuinely useful, including projects that solve problems in my own life and projects that can eventually become something larger.

I also want financial independence. I want to create businesses, develop useful skills, and build multiple sources of capability and opportunity rather than depending on a single path.

Beyond work and technology, I want to continue learning practical skills, exploring the world, and experiencing things outside a screen.

Most importantly, I want to become someone I can respect. Not someone who is perfect, but someone who keeps moving, learns from mistakes, and takes responsibility for the direction of his life.

STILL BECOMING

The future is not written yet.

I do not know exactly where life will take me. I only know the direction I want to move in. The rest will be shaped by what I learn, what I build, the choices I make, and the people I meet along the way.
