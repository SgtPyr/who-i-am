MY HOBBIES

What I enjoy doing.

Not everything has to be productive. Some things are worth doing simply because they make life more interesting.

OUTSIDE THE SERIOUS STUFF

The things I come back to when I am not building or learning.

Fishing is one of the hobbies I enjoy most. Being outside, observing the water, choosing equipment, learning about different techniques, and waiting for a bite gives me a completely different kind of experience from sitting in front of a screen.

Martial arts is another important hobby for me. Training taught me to appreciate repetition, discipline, physical control, competition, and the uncomfortable process of improving something through practice.

I also enjoy experimenting with technology. Building small programs, testing ideas, creating tools, and figuring out why something does not work can easily become a hobby in itself.

Learning is also something I enjoy outside of formal education. I can become interested in a subject simply because I encountered a question that I could not answer.

I enjoy exploring new things as well. Sometimes that means learning a new technical skill. Other times it means trying something completely unrelated to what I normally do.

I also value quiet time. There is something useful about occasionally stepping away from constant information, noise, and activity and simply having time to think.
