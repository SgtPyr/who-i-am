PERSONAL PREFERENCES

The details people usually don't ask about.

What someone likes and dislikes can reveal a surprising amount about them. Not everything has a deep philosophical explanation. Some things simply feel right, while others don't.

I tend to appreciate things that give me room to think, learn, explore, build, or simply enjoy the moment without unnecessary noise.

I also have things I deliberately avoid. Some are personal preferences, while others come from experiences that taught me what I don't want around me.
