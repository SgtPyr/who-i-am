WHY I BUILD

Ideas become more useful when they become real.

I enjoy building things because creating something forces an idea to become concrete.

A project can start as a simple question, an inconvenience, an experiment, or an idea that I cannot stop thinking about.

Some projects may become useful tools. Others may simply teach me something. Not everything needs to become a product to be worth building.
