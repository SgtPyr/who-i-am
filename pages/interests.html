MY INTERESTS

What draws my attention.

Some subjects keep pulling me back. The more I learn about them, the more questions I seem to have.

WHAT I AM CURIOUS ABOUT

I tend to follow questions wherever they lead.

Technology is one of my strongest interests. I like understanding how computers, software, networks, and digital systems work beneath the surface.

Programming interests me because it turns ideas into systems that can actually do something. I enjoy experimenting with code, building projects, solving problems, and understanding why something works or fails.

Artificial intelligence interests me because it raises questions that go beyond software. I am interested in how intelligent systems work, how they can be useful, and how they might change the way people interact with technology.

Cybersecurity interests me from both technical and practical perspectives. I want to understand how systems fail, how attacks work, how information can be protected, and how people can build more secure systems.

I am also interested in philosophy and human behavior. Understanding why people think, act, believe, and make decisions the way they do is a different kind of technical problem, but it is still a problem worth investigating.

My interests are not limited to technology. I also find myself drawn toward practical skills, the outdoors, fishing, martial arts, and subjects that help me become more capable and independent.
