BEYOND THE SURFACE

This is how I see myself.

I am Wilson. I am a curious person who wants to understand how things work, why people behave the way they do, and what kind of person I can become through the choices I make.

I spend a lot of my time learning, building, experimenting, thinking, and exploring different interests. I am drawn toward technology, programming, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, philosophy, fishing, martial arts, and practical skills.

I value independence, competence, knowledge, privacy, discipline, honesty, and the freedom to choose my own direction.

I am not trying to present a perfect version of myself here. I have weaknesses, contradictions, unfinished goals, and things I still do not completely understand about myself.

This website exists to document that process. It is an attempt to explain who I am today while leaving room for who I may become tomorrow.
