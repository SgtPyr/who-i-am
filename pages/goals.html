MY GOALS

Where I Am
Going

The things I want to build, learn, accomplish, experience, and become over the course of my life.

DIRECTION

I want a life I built deliberately.

Goals give direction to the things I spend my time learning and building. They also change as I gain experience and understand myself better.

I want to become capable enough to solve problems independently, create useful things, develop valuable skills, and build a life with more freedom.

Some goals are concrete and measurable. Others are longer-term ideas about the kind of person I want to become and the kind of life I want to create.

Create projects and eventually larger ventures that exist beyond temporary ideas and become something real.

STILL MOVING

The destination can change.

I don't expect to have my entire life planned out. The important part is continuing to move toward greater capability, independence, knowledge, and freedom.
