RELATIONSHIPS

The People
Around Me

How I think about trust, friendship, connection, boundaries, and the people I choose to let into my life.

CONNECTION

Not every connection is equal.

Relationships matter, but I don't believe that being surrounded by many people automatically means having meaningful connections.

I value relationships where there is genuine trust, honesty, mutual respect, and enough freedom for both people to remain themselves.

I also value personal boundaries. Being close to someone does not mean every part of a person's life needs to become public or shared.

WHAT MATTERS TO ME

The foundations of meaningful connection.

01

Trust

Trust takes time to build and can disappear quickly. I value people whose actions match their words.

02

Honesty

I prefer honest communication over pretending everything is fine when it isn't.

03

Respect

A healthy relationship requires respecting differences, boundaries, choices, and individuality.

04

Loyalty

I value people who remain genuine and dependable even when circumstances become inconvenient.

05

Boundaries

Caring about someone does not remove the need for privacy, independence, and personal space.

06

Genuine Connection

I value conversations and relationships where people can move beyond appearances and actually understand one another.

PEOPLE MATTER

Connection should still leave room to be yourself.

The right relationships should not require someone to erase their individuality. They should create enough trust for people to be honest while still remaining their own person.
