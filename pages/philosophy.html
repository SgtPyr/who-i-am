MY PHILOSOPHY

How I see life.

I do not have all the answers. These are some of the ideas I currently use to make sense of life and decide how I want to live it.

HOW I SEE THINGS

Life is something I have to actively understand.

I believe that a person should question the things they believe rather than accepting everything simply because they were told to believe it.

I do not think knowing something once means I understand it permanently. People change, circumstances change, and new information can change the way an old idea looks.

I believe failure is information. Failing at something can reveal weaknesses, incorrect assumptions, or parts of a plan that need to change.

I also believe that competence creates freedom. The more things a person can understand and do for themselves, the more choices they have.

I try not to measure my entire life by other people's timelines. People begin with different circumstances, different opportunities, and different goals.

At the same time, freedom comes with responsibility. Choosing my own direction also means accepting that my decisions have consequences.

IDEAS I RETURN TO

A few principles that shape the way I think.

01

Question

Do not accept an idea simply because it is familiar, popular, or confidently presented.

02

Learn

Understanding something gives you more freedom to make informed decisions about it.

03

Experiment

Experience and experimentation can reveal things that theory alone cannot.

04

Adapt

Changing your mind when the evidence changes is not weakness. It is part of learning.

05

Take Responsibility

Freedom means accepting responsibility for the choices that come with it.

06

Keep Becoming

I do not need to have a finished identity. I can keep learning, changing, and becoming.

NOT A FINAL ANSWER

I am still figuring it out.

This philosophy is not a set of rules carved into stone. It is a snapshot of how I currently understand the world. Some of these ideas may change as I gain more experience.
