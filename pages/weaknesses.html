MY WEAKNESSES

What I Still
Need to Improve

The limitations, habits, and imperfections I recognize in myself instead of pretending they don't exist.

HONEST SELF-ASSESSMENT

I am not finished.

Being honest about myself means acknowledging what I struggle with, not just listing the things I am comfortable admitting.

Some weaknesses come from habits, some from inexperience, and some are simply areas where I have not yet developed enough discipline or skill.

Recognizing a weakness does not make it permanent. It gives me something specific to understand, work on, and eventually improve.

AREAS I AM WORKING ON

Things I still need to work on.

01

Consistency

Starting something is easier than maintaining the same level of effort over a long period of time.

02

Overthinking

Thinking deeply can be useful, but sometimes too much analysis delays actually doing something.

03

Patience

I can become frustrated when progress is slower than I expect or when a problem refuses to cooperate.

04

Focus

Having many interests can make it difficult to decide which direction deserves attention first.

05

Asking for Help

Independence can become a weakness when I spend too much time trying to solve something entirely by myself.

06

Impatience With Myself

I can expect myself to understand or accomplish something faster than is realistically possible.

WORK IN PROGRESS

Weaknesses are not the whole story.

I don't want to hide my weaknesses, but I also don't want them to become excuses. They are part of where I am today, not necessarily where I will remain.
