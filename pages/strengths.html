MY STRENGTHS

What I Am
Good At

The abilities, qualities, and ways of thinking that help me learn, solve problems, adapt, and keep moving forward.

KNOWING MYSELF

Strength isn't always obvious.

Strengths are not limited to things someone can put on a resume. Some of them appear in the way a person thinks, learns, reacts to problems, or handles difficult situations.

I recognize that some of my strengths come from curiosity, persistence, independence, and the willingness to keep learning when I don't understand something.

These qualities are not fixed abilities. They can become stronger through practice, experience, failure, and deliberate effort.

STILL DEVELOPING

Strengths can be trained.

Being good at something today does not mean there is nothing left to improve. I see strengths as abilities that can continue developing through experience, discipline, and deliberate practice.
