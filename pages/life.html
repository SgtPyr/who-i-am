THE BIGGER PICTURE

A person is built over time.

Who I am today did not appear all at once. It comes from experiences, interests, mistakes, relationships, decisions, and the things I chose to keep learning.

Some parts of my life have been intentional. Others simply happened. Both have contributed to the person I am still becoming.

This page is about that bigger picture: the parts of life that don't always fit neatly into a resume or a short introduction.
