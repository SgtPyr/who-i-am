Some questions are easy to answer. Others require more thought. These are the questions that reveal the person beyond the surface.

LOOKING DEEPER

The questions people rarely ask.

A short introduction can tell you a person's name, interests, education, or occupation. It cannot tell you everything that makes someone who they are.

These questions go deeper into the things that shape a person: beliefs, experiences, fears, ambitions, relationships, habits, contradictions, and the way they see the world.

Some answers may change over time. That is part of the point. Knowing yourself is not a one-time task.
